HITRUST Certification Roadmap

How I took a healthcare company from no documented processes to HITRUST certified

HITRUST is a security and privacy certification widely used in U.S. healthcare. When I started at Ontrak Health, the company had no documented processes and few people knew what HITRUST required. I taught myself the framework, led the effort, and the company became certified. I was then promoted to lead information security and then promoted again while in that role.

The roadmap

Phase 1

Learn the requirements

I worked with the HITRUST audit firm to define which requirements applied to the company and became the in-house expert on each requirement statement.

Phase 2

Document how work gets done

I created templates and worked with people at every level to write down what they actually did. Department leads adopted them, and the documented processes became each department's standard.

Phase 3

Write the policies and procedures

I wrote all the policies and standard operating procedures HITRUST required, with 7 departments: IT, software engineering, HR, operations, finance, legal and information security.

Phase 4

Build what was missing

Where a requirement had no process behind it, I designed a new one and put it in place.

Phase 5

Set up governance

I created a governance program so the controls kept working after the audit, not just during it.

Phase 6

Train everyone

I created the training content and trained all 230 employees.

Phase 7

Drive the audit

I ran the compliance and audit effort with the audit firm. Result: HITRUST certified.

What I learned

  • Document reality first. Writing down how people already work gets buy-in faster than handing them a new rulebook.
  • Templates turn one-off work into standards. Once a department used a template, the process became the standard.
  • Own the requirements. Knowing every requirement statement myself meant I could answer the auditors' questions and decide what each team had to do.
  • Certification is a starting point. The governance program and training are what keep a company compliant the following years for recertification.
